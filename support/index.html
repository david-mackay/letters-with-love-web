Before you write

These details help us resolve issues faster:

  • The email or username you use to sign in
  • Your device model and iOS version
  • A short description of what happened and what you expected
  • Screenshots, if something looks wrong on screen

Common topics

  • Account & sign-in — password reset and verification are handled through the sign-in flow in the app.
  • Pen pals — both people need an account. Search by username to send a letter.
  • Ember letters — the countdown starts when the recipient opens the letter. After it expires, the letter is removed.
  • Delete account — you can delete your account from Profile in the app. This removes your profile and your side of stored letters.
